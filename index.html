About the Firm

A quiet, dependable office behind your paperwork.

Edwin Incorporation exists to take the weight of routine office work off your desk. We prepare and format documents in MS Word, enter and clean data with care, and assist with the drafting and assembly of research papers — so that what reaches you is polished, correct and ready to use.

Every assignment, large or small, is treated with the same standard: careful typing, accurate figures, clean formatting and strict confidentiality.

Our working principles

  • ◆Accuracy first. Work is checked before it is returned.
  • ◆Confidentiality. Your documents and data stay with us alone.
  • ◆Clear turnaround. Deadlines agreed upfront, and met.
  • ◆Fair employment. We hire and train our own operators.
What We Do

Our Services

Data Entry Services

General office support on the computer — typing, formatting, record keeping and routine documentation tasks carried out reliably.
